CHAPTER 1

The Cobbler’s Son

Chapter 1구두장이의 아들

Tommy Stubbins is nine and a half years old. He lives in a small town by a river. He loves to watch the ships and sing with the sailors, and he dreams of sailing out into the world one day. His best friends are Joe, the mussel-man, and Matthew, the cat’s-meat-man.아홉 살 반 토미 스터빈스는 강가 작은 마을에 살아요. 배를 구경하며 선원들의 노래를 따라 부르는 걸 좋아하고, 언젠가 넓은 세상으로 떠나는 꿈을 꿔요. 홍합 잡는 조 할아버지와 고양이 먹이 파는 매튜 아저씨가 가장 친한 친구예요.

1Words to Know꼭 알아야 할 단어

These words show up in many other books, too. Read them aloud before you read the chapter.이 책 밖에서도 자주 만나는 단어예요. 장을 읽기 전에 소리 내어 읽어 보세요.

long (to)verb
Meaningto want something very much몹시 바라다, 간절히 원하다
In the book“For I longed always to sail away with those brave ships”
ExampleOn rainy days, she longed to play outside.비 오는 날이면 그 아이는 밖에서 너무 놀고 싶었어요.
TipIt looks like the word long (not short), but here it means “to want.”‘길다’의 long과 철자가 같지만 여기서는 ‘바라다’라는 뜻이에요.
pretendverb
Meaningto act as if something is true when it is not~인 척하다
In the book“…pretending to myself that I too was a sailor.”
ExampleMy sister pretends to be a cat when she plays.동생은 놀 때 고양이인 척해요.
foreignadjective
Meaningfrom another country외국의, 다른 나라의
In the book“…to seek my fortune in foreign lands”
ExampleWe tried foreign food at the festival.축제에서 외국 음식을 먹어 봤어요.
marvelousadjective
Meaningwonderful; amazing놀라운, 정말 멋진
In the book“This old man was simply marvelous at making things.”
ExampleThe fireworks last night were marvelous!어젯밤 불꽃놀이는 정말 멋졌어요!
cleveradjective
Meaningquick to learn; good at doing things영리한, 솜씨 좋은
In the book“I never saw a man so clever with his hands.”
ExampleThe clever fox found a way out of the box.영리한 여우가 상자에서 나갈 방법을 찾았어요.
Tipclever with your hands = good at making or fixing thingsclever with one’s hands = 손재주가 좋은
mendverb
Meaningto fix something that is broken or torn고치다, 수선하다
In the book“He used to mend my toy ships for me”
ExampleGrandma can mend a torn shirt in five minutes.할머니는 찢어진 셔츠를 5분 만에 고치실 수 있어요.
fond ofadjective phrase
Meaningliking someone or something very much~을 아주 좋아하는
In the book“But I was extremely fond of animals.”
ExampleMy dad is very fond of spicy noodles.아빠는 매운 국수를 정말 좋아하세요.
rambleverb
Meaningto walk around for fun, without a set path(정해진 길 없이) 이리저리 거닐다
In the book“…rambling through the countryside after blackberries”
ExampleWe rambled through the park and found a hidden pond.우리는 공원을 이리저리 거닐다가 숨은 연못을 발견했어요.
Story words이야기에만 나오는 말

2Check Your Understanding내용 이해 질문

  1. What does Tommy’s father do?토미의 아빠는 무슨 일을 하나요?
    • ① He is a sailor.
    • ② He is a cobbler.
    • ③ He is a fisherman.
  2. What did Tommy often do on the river-wall?토미가 강둑에서 자주 한 일은 무엇인가요?
    • ① He sang along with the sailors.
    • ② He did his homework.
    • ③ He sailed a ship by himself.
  3. Why didn’t Tommy go to school?토미가 학교에 다니지 않은 이유는 무엇인가요?
    • ① The school was too far away.
    • ② His father could not pay for school.
    • ③ He didn’t like studying.
  4. What is Joe, the mussel-man, good at? Write one thing.조 할아버지는 무엇을 잘하나요? 한 가지를 써 보세요.

3Think & Write생각해 볼 질문 · 써 보기

Talk about it Tommy says he did not know how happy he was back then. Is there something in your life now that you might miss when you grow up?토미는 그때가 얼마나 행복했는지 몰랐다고 해요. 지금 내 생활 중에 크면 그리워질 것 같은 일이 있나요?

Write Is there a faraway place you would like to sail to, like Tommy? Write 2–3 sentences about where you want to go and what you want to do there.토미처럼 배를 타고 가 보고 싶은 먼 곳이 있나요? 어디에 가서 무엇을 하고 싶은지 2~3문장으로 써 보세요.

I want to sail to ______ because ______.

CHAPTER 2

I Hear of the Great Naturalist

Chapter 2위대한 박물학자 이야기를 듣다

Tommy saves a squirrel from a hawk, but its legs are hurt. Joe says only one man can help: Doctor John Dolittle. On the way to the Doctor’s house, Matthew tells Tommy an amazing secret. The Doctor can talk to animals!토미가 매에게 잡힌 다람쥐를 구했지만 다리가 다쳤어요. 조 할아버지는 도와줄 수 있는 사람은 둘리틀 선생님뿐이래요. 선생님 집에 가는 길에 매튜 아저씨가 놀라운 비밀을 알려 줘요. 선생님은 동물과 말할 수 있대요!

1Words to Know꼭 알아야 할 단어

These words show up in many other books, too. Read them aloud before you read the chapter.이 책 밖에서도 자주 만나는 단어예요. 장을 읽기 전에 소리 내어 읽어 보세요.

examineverb
Meaningto look at something very carefully자세히 살펴보다, 검사하다
In the book“Joe put on his spectacles and examined it carefully.”
ExampleThe doctor examined my throat with a small light.의사 선생님이 작은 불빛으로 내 목을 자세히 살펴보셨어요.
naturalistnoun
Meaninga person who studies animals, plants and nature자연을 연구하는 사람, 박물학자
In the book“Doctor Dolittle is a nacheralist.”
ExampleThe naturalist wrote down the name of every bird she saw.박물학자는 자기가 본 새의 이름을 하나하나 적었어요.
TipIn the book, Joe says nacheralist. That is how he speaks. The correct spelling is naturalist.책 속 nacheralist는 조 할아버지의 사투리예요. 바른 철자는 naturalist예요.
voyagenoun
Meaninga long trip, usually by ship(배를 타고 가는) 긴 여행, 항해
In the book“He’s away on a voyage.”
ExampleThe voyage across the ocean took two whole weeks.바다를 건너는 항해는 꼬박 2주가 걸렸어요.
TipIt is in the title of this book: The Voyages of Doctor Dolittle.이 책 제목에도 나와요.
discoverynoun
Meaningfinding something new for the first time발견
In the book“He’s made some great voyages and some wonderful discoveries.”
ExampleFinding the old map was an exciting discovery.오래된 지도를 찾은 건 신나는 발견이었어요.
Tipdiscover (verb) → discovery (noun)discover(발견하다)의 명사형이에요.
patientlyadverb
Meaningcalmly, without getting upset while waiting참을성 있게
In the book“…a whole procession of dogs following us patiently.”
ExampleThe puppy waited patiently for its dinner.강아지가 저녁밥을 얌전히 기다렸어요.
certainlyadverb
Meaningof course; surely물론이지요, 확실히
In the book“Certainly,” said the cat’s-meat-man.
Example“Can you help me?” “Certainly!”"도와줄 수 있어?" "물론이지!"
whisperverb
Meaningto speak very quietly속삭이다
In the book“The cat’s-meat-man stopped and leant down to whisper in my ear.”
ExampleShe whispered the secret so no one else could hear.그 아이는 아무도 못 듣게 비밀을 속삭였어요.
mysteriousadjective
Meaningstrange and hard to explain신비한, 수수께끼 같은
In the book“…he said in a hoarse, mysterious voice.”
ExampleA mysterious box appeared on our doorstep.우리 집 문 앞에 수수께끼 같은 상자가 놓여 있었어요.
Story words이야기에만 나오는 말
hawk a large bird that hunts매claw a sharp nail on an animal’s foot발톱squirrel a small animal with a bushy tail다람쥐spectacles an old word for glasses안경surgeon a doctor who does operations외과 의사

2Check Your Understanding내용 이해 질문

  1. What animal did Tommy save?토미가 구한 동물은 무엇인가요?
    • ① a hawk
    • ② a squirrel
    • ③ a puppy
  2. Why couldn’t Joe fix the squirrel himself?조 할아버지가 다람쥐를 직접 고치지 못한 이유는 무엇인가요?
    • ① He was too busy.
    • ② He didn’t have the tools or the knowledge.
    • ③ He didn’t like squirrels.
  3. Where was the Doctor when Tommy and Matthew got to his house?토미와 매튜 아저씨가 집에 갔을 때 선생님은 어디에 있었나요?
    • ① He was sleeping inside.
    • ② He was away on a voyage.
    • ③ He was at the market.
  4. What secret did Matthew whisper about Doctor Dolittle?매튜 아저씨가 귓속말로 알려준 둘리틀 선생님의 비밀은 무엇인가요?

3Think & Write생각해 볼 질문 · 써 보기

Talk about it Matthew keeps the Doctor’s secret because people might laugh. Have you ever kept a friend’s secret? Why is keeping a secret important?매튜 아저씨는 사람들이 웃을까 봐 선생님의 비밀을 지켜요. 친구의 비밀을 지켜 준 적이 있나요? 비밀을 지키는 건 왜 중요할까요?

Write If you could talk to animals, which animal would you talk to? What would you ask? Write 2–3 sentences.동물과 말할 수 있다면 어떤 동물에게 무엇을 물어보고 싶나요? 2~3문장으로 써 보세요.

If I could talk to a ______, I would ask, “______?”

Answer Key

Chapter 1

  • 1 ② · 2 ① · 3 ②
  • 4 e.g. He is very good at making things. He fixes Tommy’s toy ships and makes kites from old umbrellas.
  • Write e.g. I want to sail to Africa because I want to see real elephants. I would take lots of pictures.

Chapter 2

  • 1 ② · 2 ② · 3 ②
  • 4 e.g. He can talk to animals. He understands their language.
  • Write e.g. If I could talk to a dog, I would ask, “What do you dream about?” I think it dreams about bones.

There is no single right answer for “Talk about it” and “Write.” The goal is to give reasons.Talk about it과 Write는 정답이 없어요. 이유를 들어 말하고 쓰는 게 목표예요.
